# Bounded training on the pilot policy's states

The first two-turn pilot learned from states generated by a practical heuristic.
When deployed at every decision, its distribution changed: it repeatedly chose
Freeze and performed worse than the heuristic on the small conditional test.
Increasing the same dataset would not directly test that failure.

`config/two-turn-training-job.json` declares one follow-up iteration. Its frozen
checkpoint chooses the behavior actions used to collect new training states.
Every shortlisted action at those states is valued by actual two-combat
rollouts with practical continuation. This preserves the value of multistep
actions such as buying and then playing a minion. It is a declared combination
of learned behavior and heuristic labels, not pure self-play. The ranker then
warm-starts from a separate copy of the exact checkpoint. The source policy,
normalization, optimizer state and random state are preserved.

The fixed settings are 80 trajectory attempts, four sampled rollouts per
candidate, 32 fresh evaluation episode seeds and eight combat samples per
policy. The two candidate checkpoints use five and fifteen additional epochs;
validation selects between them before any new full-policy test evaluation.
The learner acts at every player-zero decision in evaluation. Opponents remain
the same practical heuristic. Unsupported candidates reject the whole training
trajectory, and unsupported test outcomes reject the paired episode with
failure counts reported. No model is automatically promoted.

The new seed is `202609062`. Whole episodes and all their counterfactual siblings
stay together across splits. The old pilot's test labels are not reused. The
feature schema, checkpoint SHA256, current ruleset and timing profile must match
before training starts. Live-source checks also run before and after the work.
One action per second, slower-action delays and the five-second reserve remain
mandatory. The fixture's 15/20/30-second windows are explicit synthetic inputs,
not a claimed table of client turn durations.

The **Bounded two-turn policy improvement** workflow starts when this exact job
configuration changes on `main`, or through a manual dispatch. Ordinary code
commits do not retrigger it. The training process has a 6,600-second bound and
the whole job a 120-minute limit. Its repository permissions are read-only.
Artifacts retain checkpoints, raw compressed trajectories, source hashes,
evaluation results and any partial failure evidence for 90 days. Compact results
also appear in the logs and step summary. No successor is automatically started.

Inspect the declared command without launching compute:

```bash
python scripts/run_two_turn_job.py --engine-root ../research/HSBRSIM \
  --out /absolute/new/two-turn-run --dry-run
```

This remains a small two-turn experiment. It cannot establish full-game strength,
human MMR, long-term upgrade value, or adaptation speed after a seasonal rotation.
Read the [pilot evidence and remaining limitations](two-turn-recruit-pilot.md)
and [transition boundaries](opening-transition.md) before extending its scope.
